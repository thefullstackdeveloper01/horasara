not get repeated after each 12 year's Cycle. Other Planets also Progress in

Fundamentals of Nadi Astrology: Progression Jupiter (3)
Difference between Progression and Transit of Jupiter:
Transit is the actual movement of a Planet as seen from the Earth. In
Transit, Planets: Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn go retrograde
also. Because of Retrogression periods, these Planets take more time to
transit a Sign, in Comparison of the actual time they take to transit a Sign
in Direct Motion. For example, Jupiter in Leo, entered on 8 Aug 2015 and
reached at the end of Leo sign on 8 Jan 2016 i.e. just within 5 months. But,
because of Retrogression on 8 Jan 2016, Jupiter started moving back (not
real) and will leave Leo in Aug 2016 i.e. approximate in 1 year. Same like
this, other Planets take their own time. Sun and Moon always move direct,
while Rahu & Ketu .... always remain retrograde.
Note: Planet's Speed is crucial factor as per astrological point of view (not
astronomical), which also need a close attention.

On the other hand, Progression is quite different from actual Transit (or we
can say Live Transit). Under Progression, there are different models of
application. As per study of various Nadi Texts, I first simply consider the
Total duration or approximate time taken by a Planet to traverse whole
Zodiac i.e. 12 Sign, like Saturn takes 30 years, Jupiter takes 12 year, Rahu
& Ketu take 18 years, Sun takes 1 year and so on.
Jupiter takes 12 years to traverse though 12 Sign, here we consider 1 year
per 1 sign. Further, with in a Sign, which compromise 30 Degrees span ....
Jupiter takes 1 month to traverse 2 Degree 30 minutes. This way, we can
calculate the Progression of Jupiter within Sign for Monthly basis. Further
we can sub-divide this Progression up to Minutes or Seconds. For easy
reference, one may incorporate D12 Chart formula.
Starting Point of Jupiter’s Progression will be the exact Degrees at which
Natal Jupiter is located in birth chart. This way, one may use Jupiter’s
Progression for Each Cycle (of 12 years duration). Same method can also
be applied for other Planet's

and Transit, one can analyse some points from a horoscope effectively with
the help of Progression of these said planets.

Jeeva Karka. Further on Gender base, Masculine characteristics are
governed by Mars and Feminine characteristics are governed by Venus.
So, for Male nativity, one should look on: Jupiter + Mars & for female
nativity, one should look on: Jupiter + Venus. This point is helpful to
understand and analyse the about Twin Birth.

How to Progress Jupiter?
Simple approach is to look on: Natal Jupiter’s Sign and Degrees in Lagna
Chart. Jupiterian Progression starts from the Sign, in which natal Jupiter is
sitting in Lagna Chart and its duration is supposed to be of 12 years i.e. Per
Cycle per 30 degree per 12 Year (or 2 V2 Degree per year).
So, first of all, need to check the degree of natal Jupiter and check how
much Degrees Jupiter had already transited at the time of birth. This point
will act as the Base/Starting point of Progression. For example, natal
Jupiter is at 5 Deg in Aries, and then 25 Deg are pending to transit after
birth. Here, remaining 25 deg will be transited
months (approx).
But as per Progression, 25 Degrees will represent 10 years of future
Progression within same Sign. After 10 years Progression, next 12 year
cycle will start from next Sign.

Progression? This question is for members.
