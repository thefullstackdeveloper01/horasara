• NADI_DOSHA:
  • Definition: In Ashtakoot Milan (8-point matching), Nadi Dosha occurs when the bride and groom have the same Nadi (Ayurvedic constitution) belonging to the same Nakshatra group.
  • Points_Alloted: 8
  • Effects: According to classical texts, same Nadi leads to Nadi Dosha which is believed to cause progeny issues (Nadi means vein/flow), physical/health problems, or issues in marital harmony.
  • Cancellations_Bhakoot:
    1.
      If the Moon signs of both partners are in the same sign but in different Nakshatras (and Nadis are different).
    2.
      If the Moon signs are in adjacent signs with same Nakshatra and same Nadi (e.g., Taurus Rohini and Gemini Mrigashira - though usually Nadi is checked for exact same Nakshatra).
    3.
      If the lord of the Navamsa of the Moon is different.
    4.
      If the couple has the same Nakshatra but different Padas (Charanas), Nadi Dosha is cancelled.
    5.
      If the Moon signs are mutually Sammukha (front-facing) or in mutually friendly signs.
• GANDMOOL_DOSHA:
  • Definition: Occurs when the Moon is placed in one of the 6 Nakshatras ruled by Mercury or Ketu.
  • Affected_Nakshatras:
    1.
      Ashwini (Ketu)
    2.
      Ashlesha (Mercury)
    3.
      Magha (Ketu)
    4.
      Jyeshtha (Mercury)
    5.
      Mula (Ketu)
    6.
      Revati (Mercury)
  • Effects: Believed to cause difficulties for the native, and sometimes for the parents/in-laws depending on the specific Gana (Mula, Jyeshtha, etc.).
  • Cancellations:
    1.
      If the planet ruling the Nakshatra is placed in a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) from the Lagna or Moon.
    2.
      If the Moon is in the 1st, 4th, 7th, 10th, or 8th house from the Ascendant and is aspected by a benefic.
    3.
      The Dosha is generally considered mild if the birth is in the 1st or last quarter (Pada) of the Nakshatra.
    4.
      Remedies include Gandmool Shanti Puja performed on the 27th day after birth or any auspicious day.
• MANGAL_DOSHA:
  • Also_Known_As:
    1.
      Manglik
    2.
      Kuja Dosha
    3.
      Bhauma Dosha
    4.
      Sevvai Dosham
  • Definition: Occurs when Mars is placed in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house from the Ascendant (Lagna), Moon, or Venus.
  • Severity_Per_House:
    • 1st_House: Affects personality and domestic harmony; aspect on 7th causes conflict.
    • 2nd_House: Creates family disputes and speech-related issues in marriage.
    • 4th_House: Affects domestic peace, property, and mother's health.
    • 7th_House: Directly impacts the spouse, marriage longevity, and sexual compatibility.
    • 8th_House: Affects longevity of spouse, sudden events, and hidden wealth.
    • 12th_House: Causes issues in bed pleasures, expenses, and foreign settlement.
  • Cancellations_Parihara:
    1.
      Mars is in its own sign (Aries, Scorpio) or exalted (Capricorn) in the dosha house.
    2.
      Mars is in the 2nd house in Gemini or Virgo.
    3.
      Mars is in the 12th house in Taurus or Libra.
    4.
      Mars is in the 7th house in Cancer or Capricorn.
    5.
      Mars is in the 8th house in Sagittarius or Pisces.
    6.
      Mars is aspected by or conjunct with Jupiter.
    7.
      Mars is conjunct with or aspected by the Moon.
    8.
      If both the bride and groom have Mangal Dosha (matching cancels it out).
    9.
      If Mars is weak (debilitated, combust, or in enemy sign) the dosha is considered mild.

• PITRA_DOSHA:
  • Definition: A karmic debt or curse from ancestors affecting the current generation, often indicated by specific planetary placements.
  • Indications:
    1.
      Sun and Rahu conjunct in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, or 10th house.
    2.
      Ketu and Moon conjunct in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, or 10th house.
    3.
      Afflicted 9th house or 9th lord.
    4.
      Sun is afflicted by malefics (Saturn, Mars, Rahu).
  • Effects:
    1.
      Delays in marriage and childbirth.
    2.
      Frequent miscarriages or problems in progeny.
    3.
      Unexplained health issues or mental unrest.
    4.
      Obstacles in career and financial growth.
    5.
      Sudden losses and family disputes.
  • Remedies:
    1.
      Performing Shraddha and Tarpanam for ancestors during Amavasya or Pitru Paksha.
    2.
      Offering food and water to crows, cows, and dogs.
    3.
      Donating to the poor and helping elderly people.
    4.
      Chanting the Gayatri Mantra and offering water to the Sun.
    5.
      Performing Narayan Bali or Tripindi Shradh puja at holy places.

    • Lunar_Eclipse: Moon conjunct Rahu or Ketu.
  • Effects:
    1.
      Mental stress, confusion, and emotional instability.
    2.
      Obstacles in education and career progression.
    3.
      Problems in relationships, especially with father (Sun) or mother (Moon).
    4.
      Sudden health issues and unexplained fears.
  • Cancellations:
    1.
      If Sun or Moon is in its own sign or exalted.
    2.
      If Jupiter aspects the Sun or Moon.
    3.
      If the eclipse occurs in a Kendra or Trikona and the lord of that house is strong.
• SHRAPIT_DOSHA:
  • Definition: Occurs when Saturn (Shani) and Rahu are conjunct in the same house in the birth chart. 'Shrapit' means cursed.
  • Effects:
    1.
      Continuous struggles and obstacles in all endeavors.
    2.
      Severe delays in achieving success, marriage, or career goals.
    3.
      Financial instability and unexpected losses.
    4.
      Health issues that are chronic or hard to diagnose.
  • Remedies:
    1.
      Worshiping Lord Shiva and Hanuman.
    2.
      Chanting the Durga Saptashati.
    3.
      Performing remedies for both Saturn (Shani) and Rahu.
    4.
      Feeding birds and animals, especially dogs and crows.
• KEMADRUNA_DOSHA:
  • Definition: Occurs when there are no planets on either side of the Moon (2nd and 12th house from the Moon are empty), forming no Sunapha or Anapha Yoga.
  • Effects:
    1.
      Feelings of isolation and loneliness.
    2.
      Financial fluctuations and lack of emotional support.
    3.
      Mental anxiety and emotional dependency.
  • Cancellations:
    1.
      Moon is in a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) from the Ascendant.
    2.
      Moon is aspected by or conjunct with Jupiter.
    3.
      Moon is placed in a favorable Navamsa or is Vargottama.
    4.
      Benefics are placed in the 2nd or 12th house from the Navamsa Moon.
