• YOGA_CANCELLATIONS:
  • Kemadruma Yoga:
    1.
      Moon is aspected by Jupiter or Venus
    2.
      Moon is conjunct with a benefic planet (Mercury, Venus, Jupiter)
    3.
      Moon is placed in a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) from the Lagna
    4.
      All planets aspect the Moon
    5.
      Moon is in a favorable Navamsa or is Vargottama
  • Mangal Dosha:
    1.
      Mars is in its own sign (Aries or Scorpio) or exaltation (Capricorn)
    2.
      Mars is in the 2nd house in Gemini or Virgo
    3.
      Mars is in the 12th house in Taurus or Libra
    4.
      Mars is in the 7th house in Cancer or Capricorn
    5.
      Mars is in the 8th house in Sagittarius or Pisces
    6.
      Mars is aspected by or conjunct with Jupiter
    7.
      If both partners have Mangal Dosha (Manglik matching)
  • Kaal Sarp Dosha:
    1.
      All planets are not strictly within the Rahu and Ketu axis
    2.
      Presence of a strong benefic planet (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury) with Rahu or Ketu
    3.
      Rahu or Ketu is aspected by a strong benefic planet
